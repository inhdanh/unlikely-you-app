Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy describes how Unlikely You Uploader ("the app") handles information when the owner of the Unlikely You YouTube channel uses it to publish videos.

Who uses the app

The app is a personal tool. It is used only by the owner of the Unlikely You channel to manage that channel. It has no public sign-up and does not serve other users.

Google user data the app accesses

  • YouTube channel information (channel ID and name) — to confirm the correct channel is signed in.
  • Uploading and managing the owner's videos — to upload video files, set titles, descriptions, tags, category, thumbnails, privacy status and scheduled publish time, and to read back the status of videos the app uploaded.

The app does not read comments, subscribers, analytics, Gmail, contacts, Drive files or any other Google data.

How the data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the publishing features above, at the owner's request. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Storage and security

The OAuth access/refresh token is stored only in the owner's private working folder, readable only by the owner. It is never published or transmitted anywhere except to Google's own APIs over HTTPS.

Retention and deletion

The token is kept only as long as the owner uses the app. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; deleting the token file removes all stored Google data. Videos uploaded to YouTube are managed in YouTube Studio under YouTube's own policies.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses the YouTube API Services; by using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
