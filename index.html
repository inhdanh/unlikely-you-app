Unlikely You Uploader

Unlikely You Uploader is a private publishing tool used by the creator of the YouTube channel Unlikely You — animated "what if" stories about the human body, the mind and everyday life.

The tool uploads the channel's own finished videos to YouTube, sets their title, description, tags and thumbnail, and schedules when they go public. It is used only by the channel owner and only on the channel owner's own YouTube account.

Google / YouTube access

The tool signs in with Google OAuth and requests the YouTube scopes needed to upload and manage the owner's videos (youtube.upload and youtube). See the Privacy Policy for exactly how that data is used.
